# Culture Builder: My Summer in 6 Words (August)

A quick, revealing icebreaker that sparks laughter and connection at your very first staff gathering.

## Objective

Kick off the new school year by giving every staff member, teachers and support staff and admin alike, a 30-second moment to share who they are outside the building. Six words is just enough to be memorable and nothing is wasted.

## Time Needed

10–15 minutes, scaling with group size.

## Supplies / Setup

- Index cards or half-sheets of cardstock.
- Markers, one per person.
- Tape or a display board to post the cards.
- Your own six words, written in advance.

## How It Works

1. **Distribute index cards as staff arrive.** Ask everyone to write their name and their summer in exactly 6 words. No more, no less.
2. **Give three to five minutes to write.** Encourage creativity over perfection. Examples: "Slept in. Read books. Zero regrets." "Renovated kitchen. Marriage survived. Barely." "Chased my kids across three states."
3. **Post all cards on a wall or board** labeled "Our Summer in 6 Words."
4. **Read four or five aloud at random.** Ask the room to guess whose is whose before revealing. Instant laughter, instant connection.
5. **Leave the wall up for the first week** so staff can read each other's when passing by.

## Why It Works

- Constraints spark creativity. Six words forces people to be funny, specific, and authentic.
- Everyone participates equally, regardless of role or tenure.
- It gives veterans and new hires the exact same platform.
- The guessing game layer makes it interactive instead of passive.
- Low prep and high energy, which is exactly right for a first meeting when attention is short.

## Script for the Leader

"We're starting the year the same way we ask our students to start, with a little creativity and a little vulnerability. Write your summer in exactly six words. Not five, not seven. Six. Don't overthink it. You've got three minutes."

## Facilitator Tips

- Write yours first and make it genuinely funny or genuinely honest rather than safe. The room calibrates off yours.
- Enforce exactly six. The constraint is the whole mechanism and people will negotiate.
- Include names on these cards, unlike the June version, because the guessing game is the point here.
- Photograph the wall and share it in your first staff newsletter or parent communication.
- Create a digital version using a shared slide deck if your team is hybrid or remote.
- **Bookend it in June.** The year-end counterpart is [[CB-103 - June - Your Year in Six Words]], which runs unsigned and includes students. Run both; they do different jobs.

## Display Idea

A board titled "Our Summer in 6 Words" near the staff room, up for the first week. Photograph it before it comes down so it can sit next to June's wall at the end of the year.
