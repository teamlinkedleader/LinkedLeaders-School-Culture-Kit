# Culture Builder: Liberating Structures Starter Set (Bonus)

Three small routines that get everyone talking in ten minutes without anyone dominating.

## Objective

Learn three short, repeatable engagement routines and use them instead of open discussion. Liberating Structures is a large collection of these; three of them cover most of what a school leader needs. Each one solves the specific problem that in an open discussion, the same confident people talk and everyone else waits.

## Time Needed

10 to 20 minutes each. They fit inside a meeting rather than replacing one.

## Supplies / Setup

- Paper and pens.
- A visible timer.
- A question worth asking. All three routines are only as good as the question.
- Nothing else, which is the point of them.

## Sample Prompts

- **1-2-4-All.** Any question where you want everyone's thinking and not just the confident quarter. "What is the one thing making this harder than it needs to be?"
- **TRIZ.** Any situation where the same problem keeps returning. "How could we absolutely guarantee that new staff feel isolated here?"
- **15% Solutions.** Any point where a discussion is about to produce a list nobody owns. "What can you personally do this week without needing permission or resources?"

## How It Works

1. **1-2-4-All, about 12 minutes.** One minute silent and alone. Two minutes in pairs. Four minutes in fours, consolidating. Then each four shares one thing with the whole room. The silent minute is the mechanism: it means the quiet people arrive at the pair with something already formed rather than reacting to whoever spoke first.
2. **TRIZ, about 15 minutes.** Ask the room how they would guarantee the worst possible outcome. Ten minutes generating that list, deliberately and with some enjoyment. Then five minutes marking which of those things the school is already doing. It is the only reliable way to get a staff to name their own counterproductive habits, because it is framed as a game rather than as criticism.
3. **15% Solutions, about 10 minutes.** Everyone writes what they can personally do this week with no permission and no new resources. Then they say it in pairs. It converts a diffuse conversation into individual ownership without anyone assigning anything.
4. **Pick the one that fits the problem (before the meeting).** 1-2-4-All when you need the whole room's thinking. TRIZ when a problem keeps recurring and nobody will name why. 15% Solutions when a discussion is heading toward a list with no owners.
5. **Teach them to your staff and let them run them (ongoing).** Any of the three can be run by a team lead with no training. That is the point of a starter set, and a routine your staff can run without you is worth more than one that needs you in the room.

## Why It Works

- The silent minute in 1-2-4-All is the single highest-value minute in a school meeting. It gives everyone a position before the room's dynamics form.
- TRIZ works because it inverts the ask. Staff who will not criticise a practice will happily explain how to make things worse, and then recognise the list.
- 15% Solutions produces ownership without delegation, because everyone chooses their own small action and it is genuinely within their control.
- All three are short enough to be additive rather than an event, which is what makes them stick.
- Handing them to staff distributes facilitation, so good meetings stop depending on the leader being in the room.

## Script for the Leader

For TRIZ, which is the one that needs framing:

"Wrong question on purpose. I do not want to know how to make new staff feel welcome. I want to know how we would absolutely guarantee that a new person here feels completely isolated for their first month. Be specific and be creative, and yes, enjoy it. Then we are going to go down the list and mark the ones we are already doing."

## Facilitator Tips

- Protect the silent minute in 1-2-4-All. Someone will start talking at ten seconds and it undermines the whole structure.
- Do TRIZ before you do anything earnest about the same topic. In the other order the honesty is gone.
- With 15% Solutions, do not collect or track the commitments. It becomes an assignment and the point is that it is theirs.
- Match the routine to the problem rather than using your favourite one for everything.
- Teach at least two other people to run each of them. A structure only you can facilitate is not a structure, it is your meeting style.
- The full collection is large. Three used well beats thirty read about.
