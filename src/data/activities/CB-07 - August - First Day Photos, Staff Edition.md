# Culture Builder: First Day Photos, Staff Edition (August)

The same tradition your students love, now for the adults who show up every single day.

## Objective

Build camaraderie, humanize the adults in the building, and create a shareable moment of joy that sets the tone for the entire year, all through the most universal back-to-school tradition there is.

## Time Needed

15–20 minutes during pre-service week or on the first day of school.

## Supplies / Setup

- A large chalkboard, whiteboard, or pre-made photo prop signs.
- Chalk markers or dry-erase markers.
- A designated photo backdrop: a school banner, a clean wall, or a decorated bulletin board.
- One volunteer photographer, or a phone tripod with a timer.

## Sample Prompts

The sign is reusable, or use individual printed cards with fill-in fields:

- Name.
- Role or grade.
- Years at this school.
- This year I'm excited about.
- My word for the year.

## How It Works

1. **Set up your backdrop and photo station** in a hallway, the gym, or near the main entrance.
2. **As staff arrive for pre-service week, invite each person to fill out their sign** and take a first-day-of-school photo, just like the students will.
3. **Keep it fun and low pressure.** Encourage silly poses, serious poses, holding a backpack. Anything goes.
4. **Compile the photos into a digital collage or slideshow.** Share it with staff before students arrive.
5. **Optionally share a version with families** in the first week's newsletter as a "Meet Your Team" feature.

## Why It Works

- It equalizes everyone. The principal is doing the same thing as the custodian.
- It creates an instant archive of the year's starting point, which you can compare against at year's end.
- The act of being photographed signals that you matter here and you are seen.
- Sharing with families builds community before Day 1 even begins.
- Staff who feel celebrated are more likely to celebrate their students.

## Script for the Leader

"Same thing we do for the kids, and I'm going first. Fill in your sign, stand in front of the banner, and be as ridiculous as you want to be. In May we're going to take the same photo in the same spot, and you're going to want to have this one to look back at. Also, for the record, I am holding a backpack."

## Facilitator Tips

- The energy you bring to this sets the tone. If you are willing to be the most enthusiastic person in the photo, your staff will follow. Lead with joy.
- Print a small version of each staff photo and post them in the hallway with their word for the year underneath.
- Create a side-by-side display at year's end: first day photo next to a last day photo, same pose.
- Share the collage at the first parent night: "These are the people who showed up for your kids this year."
- Catch the staff who are not in the building on pre-service day, including bus drivers and part-time staff, rather than letting the collage quietly become a teacher collage.

## Display Idea

Small prints in the hallway with each person's word for the year underneath, all roles mixed together. Keep the originals for the May side-by-side, which is the payoff.
