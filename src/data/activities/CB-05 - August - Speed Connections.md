# Culture Builder: Speed Connections, Back-to-School Edition (August)

A fast-paced relationship builder that gets every staff member talking to someone new in the first 20 minutes of the year.

## Objective

Before the first bell rings on Day 1 of staff pre-service week, ensure that every person in the building has had a real conversation with at least 5 colleagues they don't normally work alongside. Cross-role, cross-grade, cross-department connections from the start.

## Time Needed

20–25 minutes.

## Supplies / Setup

- Chairs in two facing rows, or two concentric circles for large groups.
- One row or circle stays stationary. The other rotates after each round.
- The prompt cards below, printed and cut, or one prompt per round projected on the screen.
- Upbeat music to signal the rotation between rounds.

## Sample Prompts

- **Round 1, easy opener.** What's one thing you did this summer that you'd recommend to everyone in this room?
- **Round 2, back to school energy.** What's one word that describes how you're heading into this school year, and why that word?
- **Round 3, the real stuff.** What's one thing you want students to feel when they walk into your space this year?
- **Round 4, fun and personality.** If your classroom, office or role had a theme song for this year, what would it be?
- **Round 5, mutual support.** What's something you're working on this year where you could use support from a colleague?
- **Round 6, affirmation closer.** Name one strength you've seen in this person, even if you just met, based on this conversation.

## How It Works

1. **Seat staff in two facing rows.** Each person faces a partner.
2. **Announce the prompt for Round 1.** Partners have 90 seconds to discuss, and both people respond.
3. **Rotate on the music.** One row shifts one seat to the right. Repeat with the next prompt.
4. **Run five or six rounds.** Close with a whole-group debrief question: "What's one thing you learned about a colleague that surprised you?"
5. **Capture the debrief answers on a card or a board.** Two minutes. Without this the activity leaves nothing behind, and the surprises are the most repeatable thing that came out of it.

## Why It Works

- Structured conversations eliminate the awkwardness of open networking.
- The rotation guarantees exposure to colleagues across departments and roles.
- Starting with personal prompts before professional ones builds trust faster.
- The affirmation closer sets a tone of generosity and recognition for the year.

## Script for the Leader

"Twenty minutes, six questions, and you're going to end up talking to five people you don't normally work next to. That's the entire point. Ninety seconds a round, both of you answer, and when the music plays this row moves one seat right. Last round is the one that matters most, so stay with it: you're going to name a strength you've seen in the person across from you, even if you met them four minutes ago."

## Facilitator Tips

- Keep the energy high. Be the most energetic person in the room.
- Use a timer on screen so pairs can see when the round ends.
- If numbers are uneven, create one trio and adjust timing slightly.
- For large schools with 60 or more staff, run this in two parallel tracks by hallway or department, then share highlights with the full group.
- Do not skip the debrief capture. It takes two minutes and it is the only artefact this activity produces.
