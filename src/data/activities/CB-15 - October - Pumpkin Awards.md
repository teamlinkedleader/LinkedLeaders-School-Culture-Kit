# Culture Builder: Pumpkin Awards (October)

Celebrate the season and your people with a lighthearted round of Pumpkin Awards.

## Objective

Let staff invent awards for each other. Silly, sweet or sincere, the goal is connection and laughter. It works because the awards are invented rather than issued, which means they name the things people actually notice about each other and that no formal recognition scheme has a category for.

## Time Needed

15 minutes across the week for nominations, plus a few minutes to reveal.

## Supplies / Setup

- Printed pumpkin cutouts, which cost nothing. Mini pumpkins if you happen to have them.
- A nomination box and a deadline.
- Markers or pens.
- Your own nomination, written first.

## Sample Prompts

Examples to seed the idea, and the sillier the better:

- Most Likely to Have a Snack Drawer That Saves the Day.
- Morning Ray of Sunshine.
- The Calm in the Chaos Award.
- Master of the Last-Minute Lesson Plan.

## How It Works

1. **Nominate someone yourself first,** and make it specific enough to be obviously true. A generic leader nomination produces generic nominations.
2. **Invite staff to nominate colleagues** for any fun or creative award they can think of. No limit on nominations, and no approved category list, which is the point.
3. **Collect nominations in a box by a stated date.** A week is plenty.
4. **Write each award onto a pumpkin cutout** with the person's name.
5. **Display the Pumpkin Patch** in the lounge or hallway. Optionally do a quick reveal or mini ceremony at the next staff meeting, which takes five minutes and doubles the effect.

## Why It Works

- Invented categories name the real contributions. No formal award has a line for the person whose snack drawer has rescued half the staff, and yet everyone knows who that is.
- Nominating is easier than complimenting directly, so people who would never say it out loud will write it down.
- The display keeps it visible past the meeting, and the lounge is where the tired people are.
- It costs nothing if you print the cutouts, which means it can happen every year regardless of budget.

## Script for the Leader

The kick-off message:

"Hi Team. It's time for some seasonal fun. We're starting the Pumpkin Awards, a lighthearted way to celebrate one another this fall. Nominate a colleague for any fun or creative award you can think of, no limit, and drop nominations in the box by Friday. We'll put up the Pumpkin Patch next week for everyone to enjoy. Mine's already in there."

## Facilitator Tips

- Check the list before you display it and make sure nobody is left out. Quietly write one for anyone who received none, because an empty patch space is worse than no patch at all.
- Keep the awards affectionate. One award that reads as a dig undoes the whole thing, and you are the one who has to catch it.
- Include classified staff explicitly in the invitation, or the nominations will skew to whoever is in the staff room most.
- Print the cutouts rather than buying pumpkins. It is free and nobody has ever cared which it was.
- Photograph the patch before it comes down.

## Display Idea

The Pumpkin Patch in a hallway rather than the lounge, so students and families see that the adults in the building appreciate each other. That visibility does something a staff-only display cannot.
