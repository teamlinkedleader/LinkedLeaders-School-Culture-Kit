# Culture Builder: Gratitude Graffiti Wall (November)

Turn a blank hallway wall or staff lounge space into a visual reminder of the good things happening around you.

## Objective

Make gratitude visible and cumulative rather than a moment in a meeting. A wall that fills across a week produces something a single activity cannot: staff and students walk past other people's gratitude repeatedly, which is what changes the temperature of a building in the hardest stretch of the fall.

## Time Needed

15 minutes to set up. It then runs all week on its own.

## Supplies / Setup

- Butcher paper covering a section of wall or a large bulletin board.
- Markers, plenty of them, left out.
- A big bold title: "What Are You Grateful For?" or "Thankful Hearts, Strong Community."
- Your own contribution, written before anyone else arrives.

## How It Works

1. **Cover a section of wall or a large bulletin board** with butcher paper, somewhere with real foot traffic rather than somewhere convenient.
2. **Write the title big and bold** across the top.
3. **Write yours first,** naming a person rather than a category. A wall that opens with "my team" fills with abstractions.
4. **Set out markers and invite staff,** and students too if you want, to write words, names, or short notes about what or who they are grateful for.
5. **Encourage people to add to it throughout the week.** Snap photos and include highlights in the staff newsletter or morning announcements to pull in the people who have not walked past it yet.

## Why It Works

- Cumulative and public beats one-off and private. The wall on Friday is a different object from the wall on Monday, and everyone watched it happen.
- Naming people rather than things is what makes it land. "My family" is true and does nothing for the building; "Denise, who covered my class" does.
- Including students changes it from a staff activity into a school one, and students write things adults would never guess.
- Zero cost and zero facilitation once it is up, which is why it survives November.

## Script for the Leader

The kick-off message:

"Hi Team. November is a good time to pause and notice what we're thankful for. This week we're putting up a Gratitude Graffiti Wall in the main hallway. Grab a marker and write what, or who, you're grateful for. Big or small, serious or fun. Mine's already up there and it's got a name on it, which is my gentle suggestion for yours too. By Friday we'll have a wall full of reasons to appreciate the people here."

## Facilitator Tips

- Put it where people already walk, not where there happens to be space. Traffic is the whole mechanism.
- Write the first entry and name a specific person, or you will get a wall of "coffee" and "Fridays".
- Leave the markers out. Markers that live in a cupboard produce an empty wall.
- If students take part, read it before it goes up in a public area, quietly and without making it a review process.
- Photograph the whole wall before it comes down. It is the best artefact November produces.
- Do not tally or theme it afterwards. Counting gratitude ends gratitude.

## Display Idea

The hallway rather than the lounge, so families see it at pickup. A photograph of the finished wall in the newsletter reaches the families who never come into the building.
