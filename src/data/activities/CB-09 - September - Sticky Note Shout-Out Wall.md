# Culture Builder: Sticky Note Shout-Out Wall (September)

A low-effort, high-impact way to boost morale and spread gratitude across your school.

## Objective

Set up a space where staff recognise each other without needing your permission or your involvement. Most recognition in a school flows from the leader downward, which caps it at whatever you personally notice. This puts it in everyone's hands for the cost of a pad of sticky notes.

## Time Needed

10 minutes to set up. It then runs itself for a week.

## Supplies / Setup

- A bulletin board or blank wall space in the staff lounge, labelled "Shout-Out Wall".
- Stacks of sticky notes and pens, placed right next to it. If people have to go and find a pen, they will not write one.
- Your own first note, already up before anyone else sees the wall.

## How It Works

1. **Put your own note up first.** Specific, about one person, about something most of the building did not see. An empty wall stays empty; a wall with one honest note on it gets a second one within the hour.
2. **Label the space and stock it.** Sticky notes and pens within arm's reach of the board.
3. **Tell staff what it is in one message.** Short. No rules, no limits, no form.
4. **Let it fill through the week.** Do not police it, do not count it, and do not remind people twice.
5. **Read a few aloud at the next staff meeting,** or include a couple each week in the staff newsletter. This is the step that keeps it alive past the first week.

## Why It Works

- Peer recognition is not capped by what the leader happens to notice, which is the ceiling on every top-down recognition scheme.
- Being physically next to the notes is what produces them. Recognition that requires opening an app does not happen.
- Reading them aloud later doubles the audience for each one at no extra cost.
- It is genuinely low effort, so it survives a bad week, which is when it matters.

## Script for the Leader

The kick-off message:

"Hi Team. We're kicking off something simple to brighten our days: a Shout-Out Wall in the staff lounge. Grab a sticky note, write a quick thank-you or compliment for a colleague, and post it on the wall. No rules, no limits, just a chance to recognise one another for the small and big things that make our school special. Mine's already up there. Can't wait to see it fill up."

## Facilitator Tips

- Write the first one yourself and make it specific. "Thanks for all you do" on the leader's note sets the ceiling for every note after it.
- Put it where people already stand still: by the coffee, the copier, or the microwave.
- Do not turn it into a competition or count the notes per person. The moment it is measured, it changes.
- Watch for names that never appear and follow up privately. That absence is information.
- Refresh the paper when it fills rather than taking it down. A cleared wall reads as the end of the activity.

## Display Idea

Keep the filled sheets. Reading a handful from September aloud in February, when morale is lowest, costs nothing and lands harder than it did the first time.
